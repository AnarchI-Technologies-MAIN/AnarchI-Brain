# Authority Dimensions

Status: FROZEN - SW0-002

## 1. Purpose

This contract defines the constitutional dimensions, boundaries, and propagation rules of authority within the AnarchI Brain.

It is subordinate to the frozen meanings established by AnarchI Semantic Language v1.

Authority is explicit, bounded, attributable, multidimensional, and non-transitive except where an applicable governing contract explicitly permits a bounded derivation or delegation.

No identity, organ, role, component, model, administrator, storage substrate, or execution environment possesses constitutional authority merely because it exists, observes state, controls infrastructure, performs computation, or is capable of an operation.

## 2. Authority Grant Model

An authority grant binds an identified grantee to an identified authority dimension under an applicable authority root.

A complete grant must explicitly bind, or have its governing authority root explicitly define, every consequential restriction required for interpretation.

Relevant authority coordinates include:

- grantee
- authority dimension
- governed operation or operation class
- subject, artifact class, or governed domain
- scope
- authority root
- conditions
- temporal bounds
- delegation policy
- revocation policy

An omitted coordinate must not silently mean unrestricted authority.

A governing authority root may explicitly define a coordinate as unrestricted only where the applicable constitutional contracts permit that breadth.

Possession of one authority grant does not imply possession of another.

Authority is not inferred from:

- identity name
- organ name
- role name
- administrative access
- observation
- proposal
- qualification
- cognition
- capability
- storage presence
- successful execution
- canonical membership

## 3. Authority Dimensions

### Observe

Observe authority permits access to identified state or material for perception, inspection, retrieval, or bounded processing within the granted scope.

Observe authority does not imply:

- propose authority
- qualify authority
- authorize authority
- canonicalize authority
- project authority
- execute authority
- authority to disclose observed material
- ownership of observed material

Observation produced through observe authority remains subject to the semantic distinction between observation, evidence, and truth.

### Propose

Propose authority permits creation or submission of a Proposal within the granted scope.

Propose authority permits possible change to be described.

It does not permit the proposer to authorize, canonicalize, or execute that change unless separately granted those dimensions and permitted by the applicable independence rules.

For a consequential transition, propose authority and authorize authority must not collapse into a self-approving path.

### Qualify

Qualify authority permits bounded evaluation of material against an identified qualification contract, policy, schema, or admissibility rule.

Qualify authority may produce a qualification result within its granted jurisdiction.

Qualify authority does not imply:

- propose authority
- authorize authority
- canonicalize authority
- attest authority
- execute authority

A qualification result does not become canonical merely because the qualifier possessed valid qualify authority.

### Authorize

Authorize authority permits issuance of an Authorization for a bounded consequential transition within the granted scope.

Authorize authority must be independently rooted from the proposal being authorized wherever the transition requires proposer-authorizer separation.

Authorize authority does not imply authority to:

- originate the proposal
- canonicalize the result
- execute the operation
- expand its own scope
- grant itself another authority dimension
- bypass required qualification or evidence
- alter the governing authority root

Authorization outside the authorizer's effective grant is invalid under this contract.

### Canonicalize

Canonicalize authority permits the authority-governed admission of eligible material into a defined canonical domain.

Canonicalize authority applies only after every prerequisite imposed by the applicable canonicalization contract has been satisfied.

Canonicalize authority does not imply:

- truth ownership
- propose authority
- qualify authority
- authorize authority
- execute authority
- migration authority
- authority over another canonical domain

Canonicalization cannot manufacture its own prerequisite evidence, qualification, or authorization.

### Project

Project authority permits creation or release of a Projection from permitted underlying material within the granted scope.

Project authority does not alter canonical state.

Project authority does not imply authority to:

- canonicalize projected material
- modify the underlying canonical material
- execute external effects
- disclose material outside the projection's permitted audience or scope

Projection authority must remain bounded by the visibility and disclosure rules of its governing contract.

### Execute

Execute authority permits performance or attempted performance of an identified operation within the granted scope.

Execute authority does not itself supply any separate authorization, permission, capability, qualification, or canonicalization required by the governing transition.

Execution authority cannot retroactively legitimize an unauthorized operation.

Successful execution does not enlarge execute authority.

### Revoke

Revoke authority permits termination, invalidation, suspension, or other explicitly defined withdrawal of an authority-bearing grant, permission, capability, or governed authorization within the granted revocation scope.

Revoke authority is not universal.

A revoker may revoke only material or authority classes placed within its revocation jurisdiction by an applicable authority root.

Revoke authority does not imply authority to:

- create replacement authority
- assume revoked authority
- expand the revoker's own authority
- rewrite historical evidence that the revoked authority previously existed
- erase receipts or provenance required by applicable contracts

Revocation must not silently rewrite history.

### Migrate

Migrate authority permits an explicitly governed migration between identified schemas, versions, representations, or substrates.

Migrate authority does not imply authority to:

- redefine historical meaning
- canonicalize noncanonical material
- change semantic meaning outside the migration contract
- reconstruct missing authority
- bypass compatibility requirements

Migration authority governs the migration operation, not ownership of the migrated material.

### Reconstruct

Reconstruct authority permits an explicitly governed reconstruction operation from permitted inputs.

Reconstruct authority is distinct from the ability or capability to reconstruct.

Reconstruct authority does not imply:

- canonicalize authority
- migration authority
- authority to invent missing evidence
- authority to invent missing provenance
- authority to invent missing historical state

A successful reconstruction remains subject to whatever qualification, authorization, canonicalization, and attestation requirements its governing contract requires.

### Attest

Attest authority permits issuance of an Attestation within an explicitly granted subject, scope, and jurisdiction.

Attest authority binds responsibility for the assertion to the attesting source or authority.

Attest authority does not imply:

- truth ownership
- qualification authority
- authorization authority
- canonicalize authority
- authority in any unrelated domain

An attestation outside the attester's effective grant has no constitutional authority merely because it can be produced or signed.

### Administer

Administer authority permits explicitly granted administrative operations over identified infrastructure, configuration, identities, services, or governance surfaces.

Administer authority is not God Mode.

Administer authority does not inherently confer:

- semantic authority
- truth ownership
- propose authority
- qualify authority
- authorize authority
- canonicalize authority
- project authority
- execute authority over governed external effects
- migrate authority
- reconstruct authority
- attest authority
- revoke authority outside an explicit revocation grant

Administrative control of a substrate does not redefine the constitutional meaning of material stored or processed by that substrate.

## 4. Cross-Dimensional Independence

Authority dimensions are independent unless an applicable constitutional contract explicitly establishes a bounded relationship.

The following implications are forbidden:

- observe -> propose
- observe -> authorize
- propose -> authorize
- qualify -> authorize
- qualify -> canonicalize
- authorize -> canonicalize
- authorize -> execute
- canonicalize -> truth ownership
- project -> canonical mutation
- execute -> authorization
- revoke -> assumption of revoked authority
- migrate -> canonicalize
- reconstruct -> canonicalize
- attest -> truth ownership
- administer -> semantic authority
- administer -> unrestricted authority

A single identity may possess multiple authority dimensions only through explicit grants.

Multiple grants held by one identity do not erase separation requirements imposed on a consequential transition.

Where independent authority boundaries are required, assigning both grants to the same effective authority principal does not satisfy independence merely because two grant records exist.

## 5. Self-Authority Prohibition

No authority grant may silently authorize its holder to enlarge, reinterpret, renew, replace, or remove the restrictions of that same grant.

No component may grant itself a new authority dimension merely because it possesses authorize or administer authority.

No authority root may be replaced by an authority derived solely from the root being replaced unless an explicit higher-order governance contract permits that transition.

Possession of revoke authority does not permit a revoker to inherit the revoked authority.

Possession of migration or reconstruction authority does not permit creation of missing authority history.

## 6. Delegation

Delegation is the creation of a derived authority grant from an existing authority grant where delegation is explicitly permitted.

Authority is non-delegable by default.

Delegation is valid only when the source grant or its governing authority root explicitly permits delegation.

A delegated grant must not exceed the effective authority of its source.

Delegation must not broaden:

- authority dimension
- operation class
- subject or governed domain
- scope
- temporal bounds
- delegation rights
- other restrictions imposed by the source authority

A delegate cannot acquire authority that the delegator does not possess.

Delegation does not transfer truth ownership, canonical ownership, or administrative supremacy.

Delegation must preserve attributable lineage to the authority root.

Subdelegation is forbidden unless explicitly permitted.

## 7. Denial and Absence

Absence of an authority grant is not authority.

Unknown authority state is not permission.

Failure to locate a grant must not be interpreted as unrestricted authority.

An explicit denial within an applicable authority regime must not be silently overridden by a broader or unrelated grant.

Conflict resolution between grants, denials, and authority roots must be defined by an applicable governance contract before consequential execution is permitted.

Where effective authority cannot be deterministically resolved, the consequential transition must not proceed.

## 8. Temporal Bounds and Expiry

Authority may be temporally bounded.

Where a grant defines activation or expiry conditions, authority exists only while those conditions are satisfied.

Expired authority does not renew itself.

Historical possession of authority does not imply current authority.

A receipt proving that authority once existed does not establish that the authority remains effective.

The treatment of an in-flight consequential transition whose required authority expires or is revoked must be explicitly defined by that transition's state machine.

No general rule in this contract silently converts expired or revoked authority into continuing authority.

## 9. Revocation Semantics

Revocation changes the effective authority state according to the applicable revocation contract.

Revocation must be attributable.

Revocation must preserve required historical provenance.

Revocation does not erase the historical fact that a grant, permission, capability, or authorization previously existed.

Revocation does not silently invalidate historical operations that were valid under the authority effective at the time unless a governing contract explicitly defines such semantics.

Revocation does not itself authorize compensating, reversing, deleting, or reconstructing prior effects.

Those are separate consequential operations requiring their own authority.

## 10. Authority Resolution

Before a consequential transition proceeds, the applicable authority path must be deterministically resolvable.

Authority resolution must distinguish:

- identity from authority
- role from authority
- capability from authority
- permission from authority
- authority dimension from operation
- authority grant from authority root
- historical authority from current effective authority
- administrative control from semantic authority

A component name, role name, or topology position must never be treated as sufficient proof of authority.

If required authority cannot be resolved, the transition must fail closed.

## 11. Relationship to State Machines

This contract defines authority dimensions and their constitutional separation.

It does not define the complete lifecycle of an authority grant, capability, authorization, revocation, migration, reconstruction, or other consequential transition.

Those lifecycle semantics belong to the formally enumerated state machines required by STONEWALL-0.

Each consequential state machine must identify:

- required authority dimensions
- proposer where applicable
- authorizer where applicable
- independence requirements
- required evidence
- legal transitions
- illegal transitions
- revocation behavior
- temporal behavior where applicable
- emitted receipts
- replay behavior
- failure behavior
- idempotency semantics

No state machine may silently manufacture authority absent from this contract or its applicable authority root.

## 12. Constitutional Authority Laws

The following laws are mandatory:

- authority is explicit
- authority is bounded
- authority is attributable
- authority is multidimensional
- authority dimensions do not imply one another
- authority is non-transitive by default
- authority is non-delegable by default
- missing authority does not mean unrestricted authority
- unknown authority does not mean permission
- capability does not mean authority
- permission does not mean authority
- role does not mean authority
- identity does not mean authority
- administrative control does not mean semantic authority
- successful execution does not create authority
- historical authority does not mean current authority
- revoke authority does not mean universal revocation authority
- revocation does not transfer revoked authority
- delegation cannot exceed its source authority
- subdelegation requires explicit permission
- proposer-authorizer separation cannot be satisfied by nominally separate grants to one effective authority principal where independence is required
- unresolved required authority fails closed
- cognition does not own canonical truth

## 13. SW0-002 Closure Conditions

SW0-002 may close only when machine-checkable evidence demonstrates that:

1. all twelve authority dimensions are uniquely defined;
2. each dimension has explicit non-implications;
3. no dimension silently implies another dimension;
4. authority cannot be inferred from role, identity, capability, permission, cognition, storage, administration, or successful execution;
5. self-grant and silent authority expansion are prohibited;
6. delegation is bounded and non-delegable by default;
7. revocation is scoped and does not transfer revoked authority;
8. historical authority is distinct from current effective authority;
9. missing or unresolved required authority fails closed;
10. proposer-authorizer independence cannot be satisfied by nominal separation alone;
11. consequential transitions remain bound to later state-machine enumeration;
12. this contract remains subordinate to the frozen semantic meanings of SW0-001.

These closure conditions have been proven for SW0-002. This authority contract is frozen.
