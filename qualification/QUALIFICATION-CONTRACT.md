# Supplemental Qualification Contract

Status: CANDIDATE - SUPPLEMENTAL FOR SW0-010
Role: Supporting semantic contract for Cartologist and S1 qualification state-machine specifications.


## 1. Purpose

This contract defines the constitutional boundary for Qualification within the AnarchI Brain.

Qualification is bounded evaluation of identified material against an explicit qualification contract, policy, schema, or admissibility rule.

Qualification determines whether evaluated material satisfies that qualification boundary.

Qualification does not establish universal truth.

## 2. Constitutional Precedence

This supplemental qualification contract is subordinate to the frozen constitutional substrate established before it.

In particular, this contract preserves the distinctions established by:

- semantic language;
- authority dimensions;
- artifact and Receipt taxonomy;
- canonical serialization;
- cryptographic binding;
- core schema interpretation;

Resolution and Validation remain supplemental candidate dependencies. They are not frozen constitutional anchors and must retain explicit candidate/version bindings when consumed.

This supplemental contract must not silently redefine any frozen meaning.

## 3. Qualification Result Artifact

The governed root schema is:

anarchi.brain.qualification-result

The initial schema version is:

A Qualification Result Artifact binds:

- subject;
- qualification_contract;
- result.

The evaluated subject and the Qualification Result Artifact remain distinguishable material.

## 4. Qualification Contract Binding

qualification_contract must bind or deterministically resolve the explicit contract, policy, schema, or admissibility rule governing the Qualification.

The governing qualification context must be identifiable.

A Qualification Result must remain attributable to the qualification context under which it was produced.

Missing qualification context must not be interpreted as unrestricted qualification.

## 5. Result Semantics

result records the bounded result produced by the applicable Qualification.

This supplemental contract does not invent one universal Qualification result vocabulary.

The applicable qualification contract owns the meaning and permitted vocabulary of its result.

A result value must not be interpreted outside the qualification context that defines it.

## 6. Qualification And Validation

Validation Result Artifact and Qualification Result Artifact are distinct.

A VALID validation result does not automatically mean that material is Qualified.

An INVALID validation result does not automatically define the Qualification result.

An UNRESOLVED validation result does not silently become a Qualification result.

Validation may participate in Qualification only where the applicable qualification contract explicitly requires and consumes it.

Qualification must not replace validation where validation is separately required.

## 7. Qualification Context Resolution

Qualification may proceed constitutionally only when the applicable qualification boundary can be identified or deterministically resolved.

If the applicable qualification contract, policy, schema, admissibility rule, or required input cannot be resolved, Qualification must fail closed.

Unresolved qualification context must not silently become satisfaction of the qualification boundary.

Unresolved qualification context must not silently become failure of a known rule that was never resolved.

## 8. Qualify Authority

Qualify authority permits bounded evaluation of material against an identified qualification contract, policy, schema, or admissibility rule.

Qualify authority may produce a Qualification Result within its granted jurisdiction.

The ability or capability to perform evaluation does not itself establish Qualify authority.

A result produced outside the qualifier's effective jurisdiction does not acquire constitutional qualification authority merely because the result can be produced.

## 9. Authority Non-Collapse

Qualify authority does not imply:

- Propose authority;
- Authorize authority;
- Canonicalize authority;
- Attest authority;
- Execute authority.

Possession of Qualify authority must not silently create another authority dimension.

A Qualification Result must not enlarge the authority of its producer.

## 10. Authorization Boundary

Qualification is not general Authorization.

A Qualification Result does not itself authorize a consequential transition.

Authorization must not be inferred from Qualification.

Where a transition requires both Qualification and Authorization, those requirements remain independently governed.

## 11. Canonicalization Boundary

Qualification is not Canonicalization.

A Qualification Result does not itself Canonicalize the evaluated material.

A Qualification Result does not become canonical merely because the qualifier possessed valid Qualify authority.

Canonical Membership requires the applicable canonical process and authority path.

## 12. Evidence Boundary

A Qualification Result does not automatically become Evidence merely because the evaluation occurred.

Evidence admission remains contextual and contract-bound.

Where Qualification evaluates material for admissibility under an evidence contract, the Qualification establishes only the bounded result defined by that qualification context.

Qualification does not manufacture missing Evidence.

## 13. Truth Boundary

Qualification determines satisfaction of a bounded qualification rule.

Qualification does not establish universal truth.

A material item may satisfy a qualification boundary while remaining subject to separate truth, Evidence, provenance, authority, validation, or canonical requirements.

## 14. Issuance And Representation

A structurally valid Qualification Result Artifact is a representation governed by its schema.

Schema validity does not itself establish valid issuance.

Possession, storage, copying, reconstruction, serialization, or presentation of a Qualification Result-shaped artifact does not by itself establish a constitutionally effective Qualification Result.

Applicable issuance and authority requirements remain separately governed.

## 15. Historical Interpretation

A historical Qualification Result remains attributable to the qualification context under which it was produced.

A later qualification contract, policy, schema, or admissibility rule must not silently reinterpret an earlier Qualification Result.

Historical Qualification does not automatically establish current qualification under a changed governing context.

## 16. Cognition Boundary

A cognition system may perform or assist bounded Qualification only within the authority and contract boundaries applicable to that operation.

Producing a Qualification Result does not grant cognition Qualify authority.

Model confidence, repetition, fluency, or agreement does not substitute for the governing qualification contract.

## 17. Lifecycle Boundary

This contract defines generic Qualification semantics.

It does not define the complete lifecycle of every qualifying organ or state machine.

Cartologist qualification and S1 evidence qualification remain separately governed lifecycles.

No lifecycle may silently broaden the semantics established by this contract.

## 18. Qualification Non-Collapse Laws

The following distinctions are mandatory:

- qualification != validation;
- qualification != authorization;
- qualification != canonicalization;
- qualification != truth;
- qualification result != Evidence;
- qualification result != Canonical Membership;
- qualification result != Authority Grant;
- qualification result != Authorization;
- schema-valid != valid Qualification issuance;
- capability to qualify != Qualify authority;
- historical qualification != current qualification;
- unresolved qualification context != satisfied qualification boundary.

## 19. Supplemental Qualification Closure Conditions

This supplemental qualification candidate may close only when evidence demonstrates that:

1. Qualification is defined as bounded evaluation against an explicit qualification contract, policy, schema, or admissibility rule.
2. Qualification Result Artifact remains bound to subject, qualification_contract, and result.
3. qualification_contract identifies or deterministically resolves the governing qualification context.
4. the applicable qualification contract owns its result vocabulary.
5. Validation Result Artifact remains distinct from Qualification Result Artifact.
6. validation outcome does not automatically determine qualification outcome.
7. unresolved qualification context fails closed.
8. Qualify authority remains bounded to its granted jurisdiction.
9. evaluation capability does not create Qualify authority.
10. Qualify authority does not imply Propose, Authorize, Canonicalize, Attest, or Execute authority.
11. Qualification does not create Authorization.
12. Qualification does not perform Canonicalization.
13. a Qualification Result does not automatically become Evidence.
14. Qualification does not establish universal truth.
15. schema validity does not establish valid Qualification issuance.
16. historical Qualification remains attributable to its historical governing context.
17. cognition does not acquire Qualify authority by producing a result.
18. generic Qualification semantics remain distinct from lifecycle-specific state-machine behavior.

Until these conditions are proven, this contract remains a candidate.
