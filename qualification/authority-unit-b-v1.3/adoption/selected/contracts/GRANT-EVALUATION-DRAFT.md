# Closed grant interpretation and fixture evaluation draft

PROPOSED / NOT ADOPTED / NON-EFFECTING REFERENCE.

A grant must use an exact closed schema. It identifies grant identity/version, issuer principal/key and grantee principal references; verifier admission reference; authority dimension; exact operation/effect class and effect-description digest; subject identity/version/digest; tenant, consumer, job, purpose, recipient and resource class/identity/version; policy/contract/schema pins; explicit predecessor; root identity/digest/lineage; grant generation and epoch; evidence time and activation/expiry interval; revocation reference/generation/state; and explicit delegation disposition. The independently supplied reference identifies every required coordinate. Final exact field names and finite limits are selected in the separately pinned Unit B schema; omission, extra fields, wrong types and unsupported versions cannot be interpreted as unrestricted authority. Digest identity alone does not reconstruct or supply missing subject identity/version, lineage or admission evidence.

No null, empty coordinate, prefix match or wildcard implies broader scope. Observation nulls allowed by the exact schema represent explicitly unavailable knowledge and produce UNRESOLVED; they never mean unrestricted grant coordinates. A missing predecessor is represented by an explicit NONE tag rather than a wildcard. Initially delegation is the exact boolean false; booleans are rejected for integer coordinates. Interpretation cannot enlarge the issuing reference's jurisdiction or substitute another authority dimension. Under the accepted authority-dimensions and semantic-language contracts, an Authorize grant permits its grantee to issue a separately identified Authorization within that grant's jurisdiction; the grant is not itself that Authorization. Unit B must preserve this distinction without amending the accepted baseline.

The top-level authorization object represents the separately issued modeled decision. It binds exact authorization identity/version/disposition, issuing principal/key, applicable grant digest, requested-effect digest and full subject/scope/root/policy/time conditions. The grant, authorization and requested effect are three separate objects, each independently identified and compared. Missing, malformed, rejected, expired or mismatched Authorization prevents successful interpretation and preparation, even when the Authorize grant's fixture checks pass.

The issued Authorization is strictly positive: decision_disposition must be AUTHORIZE. DENY and DEFER are refusal/deferral evaluation outcomes, not issued Authorization values, and cannot inhabit this positive object. They must refuse or hold issuance/consumption; renaming a negative finding as an Authorization does not make it exercisable.

GrantEvaluation, AuthorizationEvaluation and ConsumptionEvaluation are distinct typed results. A satisfied GrantEvaluation establishes only the modeled author's jurisdiction to issue the decision. AuthorizationEvaluation independently checks the actually supplied modeled Authorization; ConsumptionEvaluation independently checks its exact requested use. No earlier satisfied layer replaces a later check. The entire canonical Authorization representation is digested first, then its separate attestation binds that exact digest. The attestation is not included in its own signed/digested subject and must not bind only an unvalidated subset. Fixture attestation still supplies no cryptographic authentication.

The exact bundle separates grant_attestation, issued by grant_issuer and binding grant_sha256, from issuer_attestation, issued by the authorizer issuer and binding authorization_sha256 plus its parent grant_sha256. Authorization.consumer_principal_id identifies the consumer; grant.grantee_principal_id identifies the authorizer, while requested_effect.grantee_principal_id identifies the consumer. effect_actor_principal_id independently identifies the actor. These coordinates are never interchangeable merely because a role label is similar.

Authorization scope explicitly identifies recipient, consumer, purpose, operation, subject identity/version/digest, resource, policy/schema/contract, root lineage, effect actor/target, conditions, time interval, generation and epoch. In this finite profile, scope containment is exact equality of the closed applicable coordinates; only the explicitly defined validity interval may narrow within its parent grant. Authorization validity cannot extend the grant's maximum validity. No generalized scope-subset, delegation or hierarchy engine is implemented or claimed. Conditions use a closed finite primitive vocabulary with exact types and defined evaluation; missing/unknown predicates remain unresolved, not wildcards or automatically true. Unsupported condition operations cannot evaluate ALLOW.

In the final exact representation, Authorization.root_id/root_digest and grant_digest bind the separately checked parent grant's root_lineage; Authorization does not carry a duplicate standalone lineage list. The lineage is verified at GrantEvaluation and cannot be bypassed by matching only Authorization root labels. schema_id/schema_version/schema_digest and explicit supersedes_authorization_id are checked; supersedes_grant_id and supersedes_authorization_id must remain the selected NONE sentinel in this initial profile.

An expired, revoked, stale, conflicted or otherwise ineffective parent grant makes its dependent Authorization unusable. Authorization revocation is separately identified and checked alongside grant revocation; matching the parent does not establish absence of Authorization revocation. No grandfathering, cross-generation survival, rollover or implicit successor exists. Initial supersession is explicitly NONE; an asserted successor is unresolved/closed rather than an implemented replacement operation.

Validity when issued and present effectiveness are separate checks. Authorization.issued_at and its issuance evidence must not precede the authorizer key's activation, parent grant activation or modeled root activation. A grant/key/root that is valid at the current observation cannot retroactively validate an earlier decision issued before its jurisdiction existed. Present validity, interval/uncertainty, current generation and both revocation layers remain independently required after issuance-time checks pass.

Grant issuance history is checked independently before dependent Authorization issuance. grant.evidence_time must not precede the independently selected grant_issuer key activation or bootstrap root effective_not_before. The grant's active interval also cannot begin before that root/key authority existed: grant.not_before must be at least those two activation bounds. A grant may be issued before its own future not_before, but this does not excuse issuance before its issuer key/root activation or permit a backdated active interval. Rebinding an entire fixture to newly selected later-activation references and observing a later current time cannot retroactively repair the earlier issuance evidence.

Four distinct modeled roles exist: grant_issuer issues the authorizer's grant under the referenced root jurisdiction; issuer is that grant's grantee and the issuer of the separate Authorization; verifier checks admission/bindings; grantee is the intended consumer of the requested effect. Accordingly grant.issuer binds grant_issuer, grant.grantee binds issuer, and requested_effect.grantee binds the consumer. Distinct names or keys do not establish effective independence. Authorize remains the authorizer's modeled grant dimension, never an S3 assignment.

The requested_effect is a separately supplied exact caller request, not synthesized from the grant or Authorization. Bind its subject identity/version/digest, operation, effect class/description digest, consumer grantee and full resource/scope/policy/contract/predecessor coordinates independently. The diagnostic consumption candidate binds both grant and Authorization identities/digests, exact effect binding and context identity; comparing a grant only to a mirror copied from itself is insufficient.

Consumer identity is distinct from effect_actor_principal_id and the identified effect target/resource. Possession of a consumption decision does not appoint its consumer as the actor or permit a different target. All three coordinates must be explicitly bound and independently checked at the relevant modeled layer. Replay binds grant identity/version/digest -> Authorization identity/version/digest -> requested effect identity/digest, together with evaluation identity, context generation/epoch and consumer/actor/target; changing any layer invalidates the prior preparation.

For this initial four-role fixture profile only, effect_actor_principal_id must additionally equal the independently selected consumer principal_id. Agreement among grant, Authorization and requested_effect actor fields alone is insufficient: a shared unadmitted actor must be refused or held. This explicit eligibility restriction does not make actor and consumer semantically interchangeable and does not identify the effect target/resource with either principal. A separately admitted fifth actor requires a new explicit profile and admission/independence rules, not an arbitrary new caller string.

Exact subject, operation, scope, purpose, roots, predecessor, generation, epoch and policy must match independently supplied fixture expectations. Finite fixture time satisfies not_before <= fixture_time < expires_at only when bounds are valid; fixture clocks are not live freshness services. Revocation, explicit denial and known conflicts take precedence over positive matching coordinates. Missing revocation/context evidence is unresolved, not non-revocation. Generation mismatch, stale epoch, interval expiry, substituted root or conflicting subject blocks reuse.

The observation time window includes explicit uncertainty; required validity must hold throughout the permitted window, not only at a chosen favorable instant. Evidence time, known principal/key generations and independently expected revocation generation are checked separately. A COMPLETE empty revocation feed means the explicitly bound fixture feed contains no listed revocations; UNKNOWN means absence of knowledge. A rolled-back feed, outdated key binding or ambiguous time window cannot satisfy current effectiveness.

Return all orthogonal findings. Aggregate diagnostic precedence is DENY > CONFLICT > UNRESOLVED > ALLOW. DENY marks a known disallowed/failed requirement; CONFLICT marks incompatible identities, state or required independence; UNRESOLVED marks missing required knowledge; ALLOW means only that all finite fixture interpretation checks passed. Preserve every finding even when a higher-precedence disposition controls. Submitted booleans and copied verdicts do not establish facts. Modeled root, identity, key and independence findings remain explicitly modeled. ALLOW is never runtime permission.

Every decision and preparation retains runtime_authority=false, operative_grant=false, effect_permitted=false, durable_consumption=false and production_ready=false. No canonical membership or effects are created. Equal fixture inputs/pins produce equal diagnostics; changed subject/context/dependency identity invalidates reuse. No persistent grant consumption, authorization issuance, revocation publication or effect execution occurs. Malformed input, resource exhaustion and failure produce no protected effects.

The closed source API is validate(raw_json_bytes, expected_sha256), evaluate(validated_fixture), and prepare_consumption(validated_fixture, prior_decision). Validation returns an immutable captured fixture or rejection. Evaluate and preparation revalidate that captured raw/pin binding; constructor or replacement-forged objects cannot serve as validated evidence. Preparation recomputes the exact decision/context and rejects a forged or stale prior decision. Exact objects and fields are selected through reference/API-INVENTORY.json and its pinned schemas. The bundle contains profile, grant, authorization, requested_effect, governance_selection, bootstrap, principals, key_bindings, grant_attestation, issuer_attestation, verifier_admission, current_observation and consumption. profile is OFFLINE_FIXTURE_ONLY. An earlier fixture without the separate Authorization or grant attestation is insufficient and must not receive ALLOW.

Required regression: remove only the authorization object from an otherwise passing finite fixture; validation/evaluation/preparation must refuse or hold, with no effects and all five operational flags false. Also attack authorization identity/version, issuer/key, disposition, grant/request binding, exact scope, policy/root, lifetime and consumption substitution independently. A valid grant must never bypass any separate Authorization check.

Required profile order is documentary bootstrap -> admitted modeled principal/key references -> GrantEvaluation -> AuthorizationEvaluation -> ConsumptionEvaluation -> effect candidate -> M14 operational HOLD. A fixture ALLOW cannot bypass the final hold or produce an operative effect. Distinct evaluation objects retain their layer-specific findings and bindings instead of flattening jurisdiction and issued-decision validity into one boolean.

Finite limits are 65536 input bytes, depth 32, 5000 nodes, 512-character coordinate text, 2048-character effect description, 64 list entries and 8 lineage entries; integers are bounded by 2**63-1, with positive versions and nonnegative generation/epoch/time. These are offline reference bounds, not production capacity or live freshness proof. Effect-description UTF-8 bytes have their own digest; requested effect is independently compared to the authorization representation.
